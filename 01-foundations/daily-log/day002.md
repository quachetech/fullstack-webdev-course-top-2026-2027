## 📚 What I Learned Today

Asking for help is a very vital skill in programming and unless you know how to ask well formed questions with full and relevant context, getting the help you need may prove to be difficult.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Prepare your question and provide code and surrounding context
- Do not ask to ask, just ask
- Before asking do your own research, breakdown the problem and research parts of the problem not all of it.
- Don't ask about your attempted solution (the xy problem)
