# Day 47 - Project Idea #6: Personal Dispensary Inventory
**Date: 13 May 2026**

**Base concept:** Inventory management app
**My version:** A personal medication management tool that helps individuals track and manage their own medications at home.

**Core features envisioned:**
- Medication intake tracking (logging when doses are taken)
- Pill count monitoring (how many remain)
- Restocking and resupply reminders (alerts when running low)
- Medication schedule management (what to take and when)

## Why this is a strong project:
- From a learning perspective it covers a broad range of web dev skills — forms, data input, calculations, conditional logic (trigger alert when count drops below threshold), and eventually local storage or a database to persist the data between sessions. It punches above a simple beginner project in terms of real-world complexity.
- From a human value perspective, medication mismanagement is a genuine everyday problem — missed doses, running out unexpectedly, losing track of multiple medications. A clean tool that solves this serves real people.
**Connection to BPS research:** Medication adherence, supplement protocols, and pharmacological support are part of the broader biological layer that BPS addresses. A personal dispensary tool could eventually integrate with the Pro-Rhythm system as part of the biological tracking infrastructure — logging supplements, adaptogens, hormonal support, whatever a user's protocol includes.
**Future potential:** Could expand into a household or caregiver tool — managing medications for family members, elderly parents, or clients in a care setting.
