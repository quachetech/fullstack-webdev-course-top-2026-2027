## 🎯 What Is This?

- Web development is basically building & maintaining websites - this is the work of web developers
- There are three types of web developers namely **Front End,** **Back End,** & **Full-Stack** Developers
- **Front End Developers:** use HTNL, CSS, JavaScript & relevant frameworks to ensure that content on the       website is effectively presented & that the user has an excellent experience.
- **Back End Developers:** use programming languages like Java, Python, Ruby & JavaScript to work with data--how it's stored & served--ensuring that the front end has what it needs
- **Full-Stack Developers:** they are comfortable working with both the front and the back end
---

## 🧠 Why Does It Matter?

Understanding the different parts of a website and how they contribute to it's functionality ensures that a development project has all the developers collaborating to coordinate the client' needs into a viable end product.

## 🛠️ How It Works

- So basically **front end developers** focus in ensuring that users can interact with the website & enjoz the experience.
- They create or fascillitate seamless & smooth user experience by ensuring that the design looks good & interaction with the elements of the site is also smooth & seamless.
- This is similar to chefs in the kitchen, they have to present the food in a way that is pleasing to the eye, while also ensuring the food tastes as good as it smells & looks.
- But this means the chef must get the best ingredients otherwise that ruins the whole dining experience for the client no matter how good his cuilinary skills are.
- Seamless & smooth front end is dependent on the back end
- fine dining starts at the farm, just as smooth & seamless user experience starts with how well the program data is stored, retrieved & served.
- A chef running a restaurant knows that a consistant & constant supply of fresh & high quality ingredients is a must, & ensures to source only from farmers who can deliver fresh & high quality produce no matter the demand.
- Good back end development should ensure that there is capacity for front end to continue delivering smooth & seamless user experience by ensuring that the program data is stored & served with full capacity for millions of users.
